# Decision rule: any unsupported claim makes a post hot_take, even if it also contains a supported claim, numbers, or a feeling.
# Check in this order: unsupported claim -> hot_take; otherwise a checkable fact -> analysis; otherwise a feeling -> reaction; otherwise other.

analysis: every claim it makes is backed by a checkable fact in the post, such as a number, measurement, quote, link or source, or a named concrete example
hot_take: makes at least one claim, prediction, or judgment that someone could dispute and that has no checkable fact behind it, even if other parts of the post are supported or it also expresses a feeling
reaction: expresses only a feeling, thanks, praise, complaint, or wish, with no disputable claim and no checkable fact
other: a joke, a pure question, a bare status report, or a reply that only makes sense next to its parent, with no claim, feeling, or fact
